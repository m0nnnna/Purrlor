Purrlor for Android 2.1 is a real app: Purrlor in its own window instead of the browser, with notifications that work when it's closed.

### What's new

- **Notifications with the app closed**, through a UnifiedPush app (such as [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh)) with no Google services. Install one, then turn notifications on in Purrlor's settings. Tapping a notification opens the right room.
- **Calls keep running** with the screen off or another app in front. The call notification has **Mute / Unmute** and **Leave** buttons.
- **Choose your server** on first launch (purr.meowops.net is filled in); long-press the icon for Change server.
- Links to your server's site open in the app.

### Good to know

- Sharing pictures and videos into Purrlor from other apps doesn't work yet; text and links do.
- Push needs a server on Purrlor 1.2 or later.

Install **Purrlor.apk** on a phone, or upload **Purrlor.aab** to Google Play.
